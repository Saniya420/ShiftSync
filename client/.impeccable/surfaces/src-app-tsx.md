# Surface brief: ShiftSync app (all /app routes) and landing

Scope: the whole client. /app is Operate (managers on laptops, employees on phones). "/" is Persuade, sharing the same world.
Run unattended: user delegated every decision ("proceed with whatever you think is best"); decision page skipped, assigned direction built.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The week is a till roll. Every list of work closes with a printed tally, and trouble prints in the roll's red. Refuses the pastel KPI-tile wellness dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Cool thermal-paper ground, white slips, thermal-black ink rail. Three inks only: thermal black (text, primary), thermal red (conflicts, late, no-show), stamp violet (confirmations: published, approved, clocked in). Archivo for UI (semi-condensed heavy for headings, like a printed shop header); Azeret Mono with tabular figures for every time, hour and amount. Hairlines, dashed perforation rules, double rule under grand totals. Positions carry a fixed colour tick decoded by a legend (raise from catalog-sleeve: hue is a code, never row order).

STORY: Manager sees what needs them, builds the week, prints it (publish) with a reviewed slip and a violet stamp. Employee sees the next shift as the whole screen and clocks in to a time-stamped stamp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: ink rail left (grouped nav, pending badges), page title row with week/date in mono, then the work: Overview opens on a "Needs you" tally of pending swaps, time off, drafts, today's lateness; Roster opens on the grid with per-day tally footer. Phone: compact top bar, bottom tabs, employee Today = next-shift slip filling the viewport with the clock button in the thumb zone.

FORM: thermal till receipt / end-of-day Z-report, candidate 7 of 7 (whiteboard rota, rail timetable, kitchen ticket rail, time-card rack, shelf-edge labels, cricket scoreboard, till receipt). seed:683356511a44e752. Raises: draft shifts sit "under" as perforated, translucent slips, published solid (from acetate manual's ply-as-rank); next shift owns the phone viewport (from vertical feed); every figure tabular, no sub-AA grey (from datamatics). Challenger verdicts: catalog-sleeve declined, high-density web competitive, acetate manual declined, ticket wallet competitive, vertical feed declined, datamatics declined.

Signature move: the tally footer: dashed tear rule, right-aligned mono figures (people · hours · cost), double rule under the grand total; publishing prints the week's slip and stamps it.
